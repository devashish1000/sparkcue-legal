Subscriptions

Plus and Pro are sold as auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions). Deleting your SparkCue account does not automatically cancel Apple billing.

Account deletion

You can request deletion of your SparkCue account and associated app data by emailing devashish1000@gmail.com from the address tied to your account, with the subject line “Delete my SparkCue account.” We will process verified requests and confirm when complete. Apple purchases remain managed in your Apple ID settings.
