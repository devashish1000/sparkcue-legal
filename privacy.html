Privacy Policy

Last updated September 17, 2026 · Applies to the SparkCue iOS app

This Privacy Policy explains what information SparkCue (“we,” “us”) collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. SparkCue is a dating reply coach: you upload chat screenshots and receive AI-generated reply cues. It is not a dating network and does not find matches for you.

01 Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive identifiers needed to create and maintain your account (such as a user ID and email address, depending on the provider and your choices). Apple may provide a private relay email.
  • User content you upload. Chat screenshots (images) and text visible in those screenshots that you choose to upload so we can generate reply cues.
  • App activity tied to your account. Cue balances, generation/confirm events, plan/entitlement state, and similar product records needed to run Free, Plus, and Pro features.
  • Purchase information. Subscription and purchase status for Plus/Pro. Payments are processed by Apple. We (and our subscription partner) receive purchase/entitlement signals, not your full payment card details.

We do not require Contacts, Location, Microphone, or advertising tracking (IDFA) for the core SparkCue experience.

02 How we use information

  • Provide and operate the app (sign-in, sessions, cue generation, cue balances, subscriptions)
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs (without using your screenshots or generated replies for advertising)
  • Respond to support and account-deletion requests
  • Comply with law and App Store / platform requirements

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your screenshots or reply cues for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

03 AI processing

To generate reply cues, content derived from the screenshots you upload is sent to our AI provider (currently OpenAI) through our backend. That content is used for App Functionality (generating your cues). SparkCue is not a saved chat-history product; screenshots and results are processed to deliver the feature you request rather than to build a long-term conversation archive for you to browse later.

04 Service providers / processors

We use trusted providers to operate SparkCue:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple; App Store billing for subscriptions
  • Google — Google Sign-In (if you choose it)
  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend functions
  • OpenAI — AI cue generation
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlement management

These providers process data on our behalf (or as platforms you interact with directly, such as Apple for payment) under their own terms and privacy policies, as applicable.

05 Retention

We keep account and ledger data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Uploaded screenshots and generation inputs are retained only as long as needed to provide the feature and operate the service securely; they are not offered as a permanent chat archive. If retention details change, we will update this policy.

06 Your choices and account deletion

  • You can sign out in the app.
  • You can manage or cancel Apple subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
  • You can request deletion of your SparkCue account and associated app data by contacting us at devashish1000@gmail.com (see our Support page). Deleting your SparkCue account does not cancel Apple billing by itself.

07 Children

SparkCue is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app includes an age confirmation for adult use.

08 Security

We use industry-standard protections appropriate to our service (including encrypted connections in transit). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

09 International users

Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate.

10 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the updated version on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
